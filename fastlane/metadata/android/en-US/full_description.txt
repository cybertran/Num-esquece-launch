Num esquece is a focused reminder app built around a simple idea: a reminder should not disappear just because you dismissed a notification.

Create reminders quickly, choose how persistent each one should be, snooze them, complete them, use recurring schedules, set a deadline to resolve them, and keep a history of what you finished.

Num esquece is offline by design: no Internet permission, no account, no ads, no analytics, no server, and no automatic cloud backup.

Num esquece is free and open source under GPL-3.0-or-later. Optional donations help support development but never unlock features.
